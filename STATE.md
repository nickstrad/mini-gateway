Goal: build mini-gateway (spec: [docs/project-source.md](docs/project-source.md), as changed by Nick's steering and his rulings) — a Go proxy that speaks the Postgres wire protocol (simple-query subset) in front of one ordinary Postgres: client TLS terminated by the proxy with SCRAM-SHA-256 login, a hard-capped connection pool lent per transaction, sharing of identical in-flight reads (never writes; strict by default), admission queues with two selectable CoDel modes and adaptive LIFO, per-tenant fairness, an adaptive concurrency limit, a retry policy that is off by default, distinct errors for "refused before anything reached the database" versus "failed after", snapshot-based observability in every component, and a static N-instance fleet configuration. No batching (dropped by ruling 3). Testing-first: the deterministic simulator lands before any proxy code.

Plan: [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) — APPROVED, 16 chunks after a foundation step. Diagrams: [docs/c4/](docs/c4/README.md) (target architecture, 12 diagrams).
