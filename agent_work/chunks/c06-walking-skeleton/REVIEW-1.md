## Release It! checklist

- **Integration points:** Every client and database wait has a deadline on the engine's virtual clock and matching socket deadlines in the shell (`docs/timeout-budget.md`), with accept-then-silence scenarios on both legs (`e2e-silent-db-startup`, `e2e-silent-db-query`, `e2e-reset-silent`, `TestTierRSilentDatabase`, `TestClientStalledMidHandshakeIsClosedAtTheStartupDeadline`) — **broken on fragmented queries** because `tClientMid` is not cancelled after message completion (Finding 1).
- **Blocked threads:** Every exit path (clean reply, error, timeout, client gone, shutdown) releases via reset or closes the database connection; `Engine.Quiesce()` and `Harness.Finish()` assert zero clients, queued, in-flight, lent, and resetting, and `TestTeardownLeavesNoGoroutine` asserts zero shell goroutines across 8 teardown paths under `synctest`.
- **Slow responses:** When a client leaves or a statement/reset deadline passes, the database connection is destroyed and counted in `database_closes` (active cancel is chunk 7); pre-forward refusals are immediate and queue/statement waits are bounded by `timeouts.queue_wait` / `timeouts.statement`.
- **Cascading failures:** Health reports `degraded` (not `failed`) via `enginesrc.CheckDatabase` when no database connection is usable and the last dial failed (`TestDatabaseHealthRuleIsDegradedNotFailed`, `TestTierRSilentDatabase`), and dials stay serialised at 1 with `timeouts.redial_delay`; however, before chunk 7's circuit breaker, requests queue up to `queue.capacity` and wait `timeouts.queue_wait` (documented in `NOTES.md` Stated limit 8).
- **Chain reactions:** Not applicable beyond the bounded FIFO (`queue.capacity` → `53M01` and `timeouts.queue_wait`): multi-instance fleet shedding and adaptive admission are chunks 8–9 and 13.
- **Dogpile:** Dial concurrency is capped at 1 (`e.dialing > 0`) with `timeouts.redial_delay` (100 ms) between failed dials, and active database concurrency is capped at `pool.connections`; every fault scenario checks post-fault requests succeed (bounded creation rate and explicit recovery-time bounds are chunk 7).
- **Self-denial attacks:** Dials are serialised at 1 (`database_connecting_peak` exported), retries are off (`RetryOff`), CSV flushes are periodic on `obs.publish_interval`, and expirations fire from the deadline heap without triggering concurrent dials.
- **Force multiplier:** Not applicable here because adaptive limits (chunk 10) and retries (chunk 12) are pass-throughs; the shell's accept-error backoff is bounded between `minAcceptBackoff` (5 ms) and `maxAcceptBackoff` (1 s).
- **Unbalanced capacities:** Every producer/consumer boundary has a named cap and full-buffer rule (`docs/capacity-table.md`: mailbox 4,096 blocking, 1 in-flight read per conn, 64 KiB client input, `limits.reply_buffer`, `holdCap`, 4 KiB reset reply, write queue 64 MiB + 2 MiB / 65,536 items, `queue.capacity`, `maxLiveConns`), though `conn.inflight` lacks a unit regression test (Finding 4).
- **Scaling effects:** Fleet config fields (`fleet.instances`, `backend_connections`, `connect_rate_per_s`, `backend_max_connections`) are parsed and validated (`pool.connections <= backend_connections / instances`, `backend_connections + 3 <= backend_max_connections`); all `enginesrc` metrics use closed low-cardinality labels only (`mode`, `reason`, `outcome`).
- **Users:** Per-client limits are config-bounded with refusals (`limits.max_clients` and `limits.max_startup` → `FATAL 53300`, `queue.capacity` → `53M01`, `limits.reply_buffer` pauses DB read, `maxClientInput` → `FATAL 08P01`); per-tenant quotas and fairness are chunks 9–10.
- **Unbounded result sets:** Streaming replies are bounded to `limits.reply_buffer` (DB reads paused at `cap - MaxRead`, resumed at `cap/2`, plus 1 in-flight 16 KiB read and at most `MaxMessage + 5` when holding `ErrorResponse`/`ReadyForQuery`), and DB loss after `req.relayed > 0` closes the client without `ReadyForQuery` (`e2e-kill-during-reply`, `TestTierRDatabaseKilled`) — **except** Findings 1 and 8 can splice `FATAL 08P01` after partial reply bytes.
- **Compounds:** Finding 3 (unsynchronised shell `EvDBClosed` overtaking `EvDBWrote` → false `Reached = No` and `08006` on writes) will compound directly with chunk 12's automatic retries (re-applying an already-sent write); Finding 2 (unread trailing bytes fed into `resetJudge`) compounds dirty-connection handling with reset verification.
