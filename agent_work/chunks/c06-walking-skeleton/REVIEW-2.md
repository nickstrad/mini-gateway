- **Integration points:** Every client and database wait has a deadline on the engine's virtual clock and matching socket deadlines in the shell (`docs/timeout-budget.md`), with one uniform boundary rule (due timers fire before events at the same instant, verified in `internal/proxy/deadline_test.go`), accept-then-silence scenarios on both legs (`e2e-silent-db-startup`, `e2e-silent-db-query`, `e2e-reset-silent`, `TestTierRSilentDatabase`, `TestClientStalledMidHandshakeIsClosedAtTheStartupDeadline`), and fragmented queries no longer trip `tClientMid` (`TestFragmentedQueryOutlivesTheMidMessageDeadline`).
- **Blocked threads:** Every exit path (clean reply, error, timeout, client gone, shutdown) releases via reset or destroys the database connection; `Engine.Quiesce()` and `Harness.Finish()` assert zero clients, queued, in-flight, lent, and resetting; `TestTeardownLeavesNoGoroutine` asserts zero shell goroutines across 8 teardown paths under `synctest`; `closeByEngine` cancels dials in progress immediately (`TestEngineCloseCancelsADialInProgress`), and `readerExit`'s `<-c.writerDone` cannot deadlock because `c.abort()` closes `c.done` and `nc` first.
- **Slow responses:** When a client leaves or a statement/reset deadline passes, the database connection is destroyed and counted in `database_closes` (active cancel is chunk 7); pre-forward refusals are immediate and queue/statement waits are bounded by `timeouts.queue_wait` / `timeouts.statement`.
- **Cascading failures:** Health reports `degraded` (not `failed`) via `enginesrc.CheckDatabase` when no database connection is usable and the last dial failed (`TestDatabaseHealthRuleIsDegradedNotFailed`, `TestTierRSilentDatabase`), and dials stay serialised at 1 with `timeouts.redial_delay`; before chunk 7's circuit breaker, requests queue up to `queue.capacity` and wait `timeouts.queue_wait` (documented in `NOTES.md` Stated limit 8).
- **Chain reactions:** Not applicable beyond the bounded FIFO (`queue.capacity` → `53M01` and `timeouts.queue_wait`): multi-instance fleet shedding and adaptive admission are chunks 8–9 and 13.
- **Dogpile:** Dial concurrency is capped at 1 (`e.dialing > 0`) with `timeouts.redial_delay` (100 ms) between failed dials, and active database concurrency is capped at `pool.connections`; every fault scenario checks post-fault requests succeed (bounded creation rate and explicit recovery-time bounds are chunk 7).
- **Self-denial attacks:** Dials are serialised at 1 (`database_connecting_peak` exported), retries are off (`RetryOff`), CSV flushes are periodic on `obs.publish_interval`, and expirations fire from the deadline heap without triggering concurrent dials.
- **Force multiplier:** Not applicable here because adaptive limits (chunk 10) and retries (chunk 12) are pass-throughs; the shell's accept-error backoff is bounded between `minAcceptBackoff` (5 ms) and `maxAcceptBackoff` (1 s).
