## Release It! checklist

- **Integration points:** Every new DB-side path has a virtual-clock deadline (`pool.check_timeout`, `timeouts.cleanup`, `timeouts.reset`, `timeouts.idle_in_transaction`, `timeouts.database_connect`) and silent-peer scenarios exist (`pool-check-hangs`, `pool-mass-blackhole`, `TestCancelToASilentDatabaseEndsAtTheCleanupDeadline`); however, `internal/pool` checks deadlines after the event instead of before (Finding 4) and `coreDrain` can misframe a split mid-message reply (Finding 5).
- **Blocked threads:** Every dirty/clean/error exit path returns or destroys the DB connection in the core, **except** `cancelDone` leaves `d.phase == dpCleaning` in the engine (breaking `Quiesce` and risking a nil-deref panic, Finding 1), and lifetime expiry during reset corrupts reset conservation (Finding 2).
- **Slow responses:** When a client vanishes or the statement deadline passes, the work on the DB is cancelled on a second connection, drained until `timeouts.cleanup`, rolled back if in a transaction, reset, and reused or closed (`no_reply` / `cleanup_failed`), all counted; refusals while breaker is open are answered in < 10 ms (`53M03`), except queued requests when a half-open trial fails or the last open connection dies while already open (Finding 10).
- **Cascading failures:** While the breaker is open, `Allow()` rejects at O(1) with `53M03` and zero dials (`pool-db-restart`, `TestCoreBreakerRefusesAtOnceWithNoDial`), and `poolsrc.HealthRules()` reports `degraded` (never `failed`), tested in `TestHealthIsDegradedWhileTheBreakerIsOpen`.
- **Chain reactions:** Not applicable here because 3-instance failure/shedding behaviour belongs to chunks 8 (admission queues) and 13 (fleet/drain); this chunk adds the per-instance pool cap and fleet budget division (with Finding 3 in config division).
- **Dogpile:** Post-fault and post-trial dials are capped by `pool.max_connecting` and the one-slot GCRA `fleet.connect_rate_per_s` (`pool-cold-start`, `pool-db-restart`, `pool-mass-*`, `pool-slots-held`), and recovery after `pool-db-restart` is asserted both in Go (`checkRestart`) and SQL (`recovery.sql`).
- **Self-denial attacks:** Simultaneous dials are capped by `max_connecting` and creation rate (`pool-cold-start`, `dial-rate.sql`); simultaneous expiries are spread by ±10% jitter (`pool-lifetimes`, `TestJitterSpreadsTheExpiryOfConnectionsCreatedTogether`); suspect siblings are checked lazily at checkout (at most 4 per checkout) rather than closed all at once.
- **Force multiplier:** Not applicable here because this chunk introduces no adaptive feedback loop (AIMD arrives in chunk 10).
- **Unbalanced capacities:** `pool.list`/`conns` bounded by `Cap`, `pool.cancels` by `MaxCancels`, `e.cancelConns` by `maxCancelConns`, `shell.cancels` by `maxCancels` (with bounded `s.deferred` fallback), `d.drained` by `drainHeader` (6 bytes), and `resetJudge.fr` by `MaxResetReply`.
- **Scaling effects:** Per-instance `Cap` and `RatePerSec` are designed to divide fleet totals by N (`pool.Share`), though `internal/config` currently fails to apply `fleet.backend_connections / N` to omitted `pool.connections` (Finding 3). No metric in `poolsrc` or new `enginesrc` fields carries a tenant or unbounded label.
- **Users:** Per-client startup concurrency is bounded by `limits.max_startup` (`TestTierRPoolReconnectionStormWithClientTLS`), idle-in-transaction holding is bounded by `timeouts.idle_in_transaction` (`FATAL 25P03`), and concurrent cancels are bounded by `pool.max_cancels` (refused/closed at cap).
- **Unbounded result sets:** Not applicable to new pool core paths beyond fixed-size reset/check/rollback replies bounded by `MaxResetReply` (`65536` bytes) and `d.drained` (6 bytes); partial-reply client cutoff in `lease.go:377` is unchanged from chunk 6.
