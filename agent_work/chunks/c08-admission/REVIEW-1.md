## Release It! checklist

- **Integration points:** Not applicable to new I/O paths here because `internal/admission` is pure sans-I/O and adds no socket reads or writes; every queue wait has a virtual-clock deadline (`tAdmission` for the rule's head sweep plus `tQueueWait` as the outer backstop), and the existing silent-database scenarios still pass with the admission queue wired.
- **Blocked threads:** The admission core holds no locks or goroutines and never holds a database connection (requests are queued before checkout, D3); on client disconnect, timeout, and rule shed, queued slots are freed and shed clients are answered immediately, **except** `Engine.Shutdown` leaves `e.admT` armed and fails `Quiesce()` when called before quiet-tenant expiry (Finding 2).
- **Slow responses:** Rule sheds and queue-wait expirations happen before any byte reaches the database, are answered at once with `53M01` and `ReadyForQuery`, and are counted by reason (`rule`, `at-pop`, `overflow`, `total`, `tenants`, `deadline`, `undrained`), **except** `Shutdown` at/past an admission deadline drops the engine shed count and records `Failed` instead (Finding 2).
- **Cascading failures:** Not applicable to database-down detection (breaker is chunk 7); while any tenant queue shed within the last interval, health reports `degraded` (`admsrc.HealthRules`), never `failed`, and shedding costs O(live tenants) with no dial.
- **Chain reactions:** Each instance owns its own per-tenant rings and rules with no cross-instance coordination, so an instance receiving extra load sheds by the configured rule and caps depth at `queue.capacity` rather than growing queues unboundedly (3-instance kill scenario is chunk 13).
- **Dogpile:** Admission does not dial the database (the pool bounds concurrency); after overload ends, `checkRecovery` in `TestScenarioAdmissionAntiBloat/{timeout,control-law}` asserts the rule returns to `StateNormal` with LIFO off within `interval + lifo_hold + interval` (1.2s) and subsequent `0.3x` traffic completes with zero sheds and latency ≤ 4 leases.
- **Self-denial attacks:** Head sweep, quiet-tenant removal (`16 * interval`), and LIFO hold expiry are driven by a single coalesced `tAdmission` timer on the engine and bounded per step by `stepBudget()`; however, per-operation sweep/selection scans all live tenant queues (O(live tenants), stated in NOTES.md limit 1) and all expired items due at `now` are answered in one sweep without rate-limiting replies (bounded by `queue.capacity`).
