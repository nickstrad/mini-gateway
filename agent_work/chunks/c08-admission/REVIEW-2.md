## Release It! checklist

- **Integration points:** Not applicable to new socket I/O because `internal/admission` is sans-I/O; every queued request has both the core's virtual-clock head-sweep deadline (`tAdmission`) and the outer `tQueueWait` backstop, and existing silent-database scenarios still pass with admission wired.
- **Blocked threads:** The core holds no locks, goroutines, or database connections (waiting happens before checkout, D3); every exit path (serve, shed, client disconnect, queue-wait timeout, shutdown) releases the queue slot, `Engine.Shutdown` now sweeps, removes, closes empty tenant queues, and cancels `e.admT`, and `Quiesce()` passes with zero in use.
- **Slow responses:** Rule sheds and queue-wait timeouts occur before any byte reaches the database, reply at once with `53M01` and `ReadyForQuery`, and are counted by reason (`rule`, `at-pop`, `overflow`, `total`, `tenants`, `deadline`, `undrained`) with `q.ShedCount() == e.snap.Shed` enforced by `Engine.Invariants()`.
- **Cascading failures:** Not applicable to database-down circuit breaking (chunk 7); while any tenant queue shed within the last interval, health reports `degraded` (`admsrc.HealthRules`), never `failed`, and shedding does no dial.
- **Chain reactions:** Each instance owns independent bounded per-tenant rings and rules with no cross-instance coordination, shedding excess work by the configured rule and capping depth at `queue.capacity` (3-instance kill scenario is chunk 13).
- **Dogpile:** Admission does not dial the database; after overload ends, `checkRecovery` in `TestScenarioAdmissionAntiBloat/{timeout,control-law}` asserts return to `StateNormal` with LIFO off within `interval + lifo_hold + interval` (1.2 s) and zero sheds / latency ≤ 4 leases on subsequent 0.3× load.
- **Self-denial attacks:** Head sweeps, LIFO hold expiry, shedding-gauge decay, and quiet-tenant removal (`16 * interval`) coalesce onto one engine timer (`tAdmission`) bounded per step by `stepBudget()`; expired items due at `now` are swept together (bounded by `queue.capacity`), and per-operation selection/sweep is O(live tenant queues) (stated in `NOTES.md` limit 1).
- **Force multiplier:** `ControlLaw` count is floored at 1 and capped at `MaxDropCount` (65,536), `IntervalOverSqrt` is exact integer arithmetic over `[0, interval]`, and adaptive LIFO is damped by `queue.lifo_hold` (verified by `TestLIFOHysteresisDoesNotFlapAtTheThreshold` and `TestAdv_9_*`).
- **Unbalanced capacities:** Every buffer in this chunk is fixed-capacity with an explicit full rule: per-tenant rings (`queue.tenant_capacity`, sheds head with `overflow`), total queued (`queue.capacity`, sheds globally oldest with `total`), tenant queues (`queue.max_tenants`, evicts oldest empty tenant or refuses arrival with `tenants`), pending sheds (`2*capacity + 2` preallocated, compacts in place, refuses arrival at `capacity + 1` with `undrained`), and per-tenant stats (`MaxTenantStats = 4096`, folds into `other`).
