- **Integration points:** In `workload`, `Tenant.Deadline` sets virtual-clock timers for both connect/startup (`c.connT`) and in-flight requests (`c.deadlineT`); `fakepg` has `FaultSilentStartup`, `FaultSilentQuery`, and `FaultConnectStall` scenarios, and `world/property_test.go` exercises them with client deadlines. `workload.Probe` has no built-in deadline (controlled by caller `RunFor` / `CollectLimit` in `world/fidelity.go`).
